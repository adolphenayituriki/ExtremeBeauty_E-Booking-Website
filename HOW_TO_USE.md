## 1. The Website and the Admin Panel

The system has **two parts**:

| Part | Where | Used for |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Public website** | The salon's site (shown to customers) | Customers browse services, book, track, send a message |
| **Admin panel** | Your site address followed by `admin` (e.g. `www.extremebeautyrw.com/admin`) | You manage bookings, messages, services, and website content |

> Only staff with a login can open the admin panel. Customers never see it.

---

## 2. Booking an Appointment (Customer)

A customer can book online in 3 steps. No account is needed.

**Step 1 — Open the Booking page.**
Click **Book Now** on the home page or any service, or go to the **Booking**
link in the menu.

**Step 2 — Choose the service.**
- Select a category tab (Brows, Lash Lift, Eyelash Extensions, Retouch, Training).
- Click the card of the service they want. It is selected automatically.

**Step 3 — Fill in details.**
- First name, Last name
- Email
- Phone
- Preferred **date** (minimum: tomorrow)
- Preferred **time** (09:00–17:30, 30-minute slots)
- Notes (optional)

**Step 4 — Confirm.**
Click **Confirm Booking**. The screen shows:
- A **booking reference** like `EB-A1B2C3` — **important!** It is used to track
  the appointment.
- The appointment summary (service, date, time, client).
- **Download Receipt** button (saves a PDF or image of the receipt).

An email confirmation is sent to the customer automatically.

---

## 3. Tracking an Appointment (Customer)

Anyone can check the status of an appointment on the **Tracking** page.

1. Open **Track Booking** (from the menu or the confirmation screen).
2. Enter the **booking reference** (e.g. `EB-A1B2C3`), **or** switch to the
   **Phone** tab and enter the phone number used for the booking.
3. Click **Track**.

The result shows the status color-coded:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| Green **Approved** | Accepted |
| Amber **Pending** | Awaiting decision |
| Green **Confirmed** | Confirmed |
| Red **Cancelled** | Not going ahead |
| Indigo **Completed** | Service delivered |

## 4. Signing In to the Admin Panel

Login uses **two steps** (like online banking codes):

1. Go to the admin address (e.g. `www.extremebeautyrw.com/admin`).
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **Send Code**.
4. Open your email inbox and copy the **6-digit code** (valid for 3 minutes).
5. Enter the code and click **Verify**.

You now see the **Dashboard**.

> If the code expires, click **Resend** and wait for a new email.

---

## 5. The Dashboard — Overview

The dashboard is the home screen after login. It shows:

- **Stat cards** at the top: Total Bookings, Pending/Approved, Completed,
  Cancelled, Messages, Services.
- **Recent Bookings** — the 5 latest bookings. Click one to open and change it.
- **Recent Messages** — the 5 latest messages. Click one to view and reply.

Use the **sidebar menu** on the left to go to other sections:
Dashboard / Bookings / Messages / Services & Prices / Site Content / Team.

The **bell icon** at the top shows recent new bookings and messages.

## 6. Managing Bookings — Step by Step

**Find a booking:**
1. Open **Bookings** in the sidebar.
2. Use the **Search** box — type a reference, name, email, phone, or service.
3. Or filter by status with the **status dropdown**.

**Change a booking's status:**
- Click the **status dropdown** on the booking's row and pick the new status
  (Pending, Approved, Confirmed, Cancelled, Completed).
- When you change a status, the customer and the salon are **emailed
  automatically**.

**View full details or delete:**
- Click the booking's row to open its details (client info, date, time, service,
  notes, status).
- From there, change its status or click **Delete** (you will be asked to
  confirm).

**Download the list as a file:**
- Click **Export to CSV** to download the current list as an Excel-readable file.

---

## 7. Replying to Customer Messages

When a customer sends a message through the contact form, it appears here.

1. Open **Messages** in the sidebar.
2. Click a message to open it.
3. Click **Reply**.
4. Write a **subject** (already filled with `Re: …` — change if needed) and the
   **message**.
5. Send. The customer receives the reply by email.

A **gold check** on a message shows it has already been replied to.

---

## 8. Adding or Editing a Service

1. Open **Services & Prices** in the sidebar.
2. To add: click **New Service**.
   To edit: click the ✏️ (pencil) icon on the service's row.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Service Name** (required)
   - **Price (RWF)** (required)
   - **Category** (Brows, Lash Lift, Lashes, Retouch, Training)
   - **Order** (position in the list — lower numbers come first)
   - **Image** (see section 9 for uploading)
   - **Description**
   - **Featured** (star icon shows this service is a favourite)
   - **Active** (only **Active** services appear on the public site)
4. Click **Create Service** or **Save Changes**.

**Hide or delete a service:**
- Toggle **Active/Hidden** directly in the table to show or hide it.
- Click the **trash icon** to delete it (you will be asked to confirm).

---

## 9. Uploading a Service Image

1. Open the service's **edit** form (✏️ pencil icon).
2. Under **Image**, click **Upload Image**.
3. Choose a photo (JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF; maximum 5 MB).
4. The image is automatically resized and saved into the database.
   - You'll see **"Image uploaded and saved in the database"**.
5. Preview it in the box; to remove it, click **Remove**.

> Images saved this way stay forever — they survive server restarts.

## 10. Editing the Homepage Content

The home page is built from content you can edit without touching code.

1. Open **Site Content** in the sidebar.
2. Use the **Live Content** tab. You can edit several blocks, each with its own
   **Save** button:
   - **Hero Stats** — the numbers shown on the hero (value + label).
   - **Hero Slides** — the rotating images/videos at the top (type, media URL,
     title, subtitle).
   - **Service Categories** — the category cards (image, name, description).
   - **Featured Services** — the highlighted service cards (image, title,
     category, description).
   - **Featured Videos** — the video section (video URL, poster URL).
3. Click **Save** after editing a block. The public website updates
   **immediately**.

> Important: these values are kept in the database. Always use this panel to
> change the homepage — do not edit code.

## 11. Editing Contact Details (Site Info)

The business details (phones, email, Instagram, address, hours, map) are used in
the navbar, footer, and contact page.

1. Open **Site Content** → **Site Info** tab.
2. Edit the fields (e.g. phone 1, WhatsApp/phone 2, email, Instagram, address,
   working hours, Google Maps URL).
3. Click **Save**.

---

## 12. Adding or Removing Staff (Owner only)

The **Team** section is only visible to the owner (superadmin).

**Add a staff member:**
1. Open **Team** → **Team/Members** tab.
2. Click **Add Manager**.
3. Enter the staff member's **name**, **email**, and a **temporary password**.
4. Save. The new staff member can now sign in.

**Remove a staff member:**
1. Find the person in the list.
2. Click **Remove** and confirm.
   - The **Owner** account is protected and cannot be removed.

**See what staff have done:**
- Open the **Activity Log** tab to see actions (created / edited / deleted /
  replied), who did them, and what was affected.

---

## 13. Changing Your Password

1. In the admin panel, click **Change Password**.
2. Enter your **current password**.
3. A **one-time code** is emailed to you.
4. Enter the **code** and a **new password** (at least 6 characters).
5. Confirm. Use the new password next time you sign in.
